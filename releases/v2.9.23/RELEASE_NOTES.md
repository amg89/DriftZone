# Drift Zone v2.9.23

## What's in this release (2025-02-25)

- **NEW: "Hide out of stock" toggle** in the Snacks Order menu, right next to the search box — hides unavailable items entirely instead of just graying them out, for a cleaner selection view during a rush.
- Works together with search and category filters — combine all three to narrow down fast.
